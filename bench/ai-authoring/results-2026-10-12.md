# Guarantee tasks under a token ceiling, 2026-10-12

Part of the [authoring benchmark](README.md), issue #270. Run
`runs/20261012T175329Z-models` (one resume; no trial repeated).

## Setup

- Model: Meta Muse (`opencode/muse-spark-1.3-contributor-free`) through
  OpenCode Zen's free tier, closed-book (`opencode.py`). It cost nothing.
- Arms: `futures` (the ecosystem standard), `script` (the `script!` sheet) and
  `lexicon` (the generated one-page lexicon alone).
- Tasks: the three guarantee tasks, `capture`, `tenant-cache` and
  `durable-retry` (`guarantees.md`).
- Two trials per (arm, task) cell, the `agent` profile, at most one compiler
  repair, one trial at a time.
- **Token ceiling: 1.2M input plus output, enforced in code** (`TokenBudget`,
  Director 2026-10-12). Spent: **455,548**. No call was refused.

## Which arm wins

- **capture:** no winner. `futures` and `script` each produced one trial that
  failed all six clauses, so their 6/12 is one perfect trial plus one zero. One
  `futures` trial never compiled, even after its repair. `lexicon` passed the
  most clauses (10/12) but no trial passed all of them. Both of its trials
  missed `a_completed_run_leaves_no_orphan_behind`.
- **tenant-cache:** a tie. Every arm passed every clause with an 11-line
  solution. The support crate carries the task, so this task does not separate
  the arms.
- **durable-retry:** `futures` and `script` tie at 2/2. `lexicon` loses one
  trial on `waiting_past_the_deadline_is_typed`.
- **Overall:** `lgwks_bot` does not beat the ecosystem standard on this run.
  `script` ties `futures` on full passes and costs more tokens per trial. With
  two trials per cell every interval overlaps, so no difference is claimed.

## What this run is not

- It used one model, not the three #270 asks for. The Director named Meta Muse,
  and the 1.2M ceiling did not fit three models at Muse's measured cost (24k
  to 32k reasoning tokens per call).
- The `old`, `new` and `fan` arms and the `aggregate`, `pipeline` and
  `recovery` tasks were not in this run.
- No human authors took part.

## Runs that did not count

Each run's folder is kept as evidence:

- `20261012T164347Z-models` was stopped after 3 trials. A stale lockfile meant
  every build was refused.
- `20261012T170130Z-models` was stopped after its first trials, at 1.7M
  tokens. The stock agent looped through its tools, one trial reaching 597k
  tokens. That loop is why `NO_TOOLS` exists.
- `20261012T172850Z-models` was stopped at 4 trials and 187k tokens. The step
  cap did not hold, and `big-pickle` reasoned for about 30k tokens per call.
